## 1. Overview
Trinity is a desktop application that scans a computer for files and indicators related to cheating. This Privacy Policy explains how data is accessed and handled.

---

## 2. Data Accessed
During scans, Trinity may access:
- file names and file paths
- registry keys
- system logs
- timestamps and file metadata

Trinity does not collect:
- passwords
- private messages
- payment information

---

## 3. Local Processing
All scanning is performed locally on the user’s device.

Scan results are only exported, shared, or uploaded if the user explicitly chooses to do so.

---

## 4. Data Transmission
Trinity may transmit scan results to third-party services (such as Discord) if configured or enabled by the user.

Any data transmitted through such services is subject to the privacy policies and data handling practices of those third parties. Cod does not control or manage how third-party services store or retain this data.
---

## 5. Third-Party Use of Data
Cod has no management or responsibility over how third parties:
- store scan results
- analyze data
- share or publish information
- take action based on scan output

Responsibility transfers entirely to the receiving party once data leaves the user’s device.

## 6. Data Retention
Scan results may be transmitted to and stored within Discord servers via webhooks configured by the user or league.

Cod may view or analyze scan data for purposes such as debugging, support, improving detection accuracy, or investigating software issues. Cod does not make enforcement decisions, issue penalties, or take disciplinary action based on scan data.

All decisions regarding interpretation, storage, retention, sharing, or enforcement actions based on scan results are the sole responsibility of the league, organization, or third party receiving the data.
